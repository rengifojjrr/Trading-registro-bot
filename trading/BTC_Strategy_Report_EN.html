1 The Strategy
Concept & Philosophy

The strategy is built on the Donchian Channel Breakout, one of the most robust trend-following systems in financial literature. The logic is simple yet powerful: when price breaks above the highest high of the last N candles, the market is declaring a resistance breakout with continuation potential.

The strategy operates long-only on the CRYPTO:BTCUSD 4-hour chart, filtering with an EMA(200) to ensure entries only when the market is in an uptrend. Stop loss and take profit are dynamically calibrated using the ATR, adapting to current market volatility.

For live trading, the strategy uses the BTC perpetual contract on Coinbase Advanced with 2× leverage, lifting the monthly return from 2.66% to 4.84% — essentially at the 5%/month target.

Pine Script v6 — Full Source Code
Pine Script v6 
//@version=6
strategy(title="BTC Donchian Breakout 4H", shorttitle="BTC_DC4H", overlay=true,
    initial_capital=10000,
    default_qty_type=strategy.percent_of_equity, default_qty_value=100,
    commission_type=strategy.commission.percent, commission_value=0.06,
    slippage=2, pyramiding=0,
    calc_on_order_fills=false, process_orders_on_close=false)

i_dc_len  = input.int(12,   "Donchian Length", minval=3,  group="Signal") // in_0
i_ema_len = input.int(200,  "Trend EMA",       minval=20, group="Trend")  // in_1
i_atr_len = input.int(10,   "ATR Length",      minval=5,  group="Risk")   // in_2
i_sl_mult = input.float(2.0, "SL ATR Mult",    step=0.25, group="Risk")   // in_3
i_rr      = input.float(2.5, "TP R:R",         step=0.25, group="Risk")   // in_4

dc_upper  = ta.highest(high, i_dc_len)[1]  // Donchian resistance (prior bar)
ema_trend = ta.ema(close, i_ema_len)         // Trend filter
atr       = ta.atr(i_atr_len)                // Dynamic volatility

long_sig = close > dc_upper and close > ema_trend  // ← Dual condition
no_pos   = strategy.position_size == 0

if long_sig and no_pos
    strategy.entry("L", strategy.long)

if strategy.position_size > 0
    avg  = strategy.position_avg_price
    sl_d = atr * i_sl_mult                       // SL distance
    strategy.exit("LX", "L", stop=avg - sl_d, limit=avg + sl_d * i_rr)

How Does It Make Money with Only 37.5% Winners?

The key lies in risk/reward asymmetry. With R:R = 2.5, one winning trade is worth 2.5× what a losing trade costs. This creates a mathematically positive expectancy:

Expected value per trade:
E = (0.375 × +2.5R) − (0.625 × 1R) = 0.9375R − 0.625R = +0.3125R per trade
With avg SL ≈ 4% of equity → expected gain per trade ≈ +1.25% of equity

In other words: even though the system loses 6 out of every 10 trades, the 4 winners generate enough profit to more than compensate. This is the same logic used by the world's best CTAs and trend-following funds.

Dynamic ATR-Based Stop Loss

The SL is not a fixed percentage but 2× ATR(10). In more volatile markets the SL is wider (avoids noise-driven stops) and in quieter markets it is tighter (better effective R:R).

Margin Call Safety — Why 2× is Secure

With Coinbase Advanced allowing up to 10× leverage, using only 2× leverage leaves an enormous safety margin. Liquidation point calculation:

Margin required at 2× = 50% of position value
Liquidation price = entry × (1 − 1/leverage)
→ BTC would need to drop 50% from entry
→ ATR-based SL triggers far before that point

The largest single loss across all 96 backtest trades was −5.32% at 1×, equivalent to −10.63% at 2×. The dynamic SL acts as a shield, exiting the trade well before any liquidation zone is approached.

Live Trading Risk Management Protocol
🎯
Maximum capital per trade
At 2× leverage, each trade exposes 200% of equity to price movement. Never add additional leverage on top of the 2× base. Coinbase offers 10× — resist the temptation.
🛡️
Mandatory stop loss on every trade
Always set SL = entry − 2 × ATR(10) at the moment of entry. Never trade without a stop loss on leveraged futures contracts.
📉
Personal circuit breaker: −35% equity
If drawdown exceeds 35% (slightly above the historical maximum of 32.19%), pause the strategy and evaluate whether market conditions have structurally changed.
📊
Monthly review vs backtest
If the real monthly rate consistently falls below 1% for 3 consecutive months, it may indicate that market conditions have changed and re-optimization is required.
✅
Perpetual vs expiring futures advantage
The BTC perpetual on Coinbase has a funding rate (typically positive in bull markets, negative in bear markets). Since we only go long in bullish conditions, the funding rate tends to work against us — factor in an additional 0.01–0.03% per 8 hours when a position is open as an extra cost.
